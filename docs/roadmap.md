# Roadmap

Progress follows evidence gates rather than target dates.

1. **Repository foundation — complete.** Public structure, contribution rules and evidence boundaries are recorded.
2. **Nominal Simulink package — complete.** Original model, source, defined validation scenarios and September rerun record remain available.
3. **Hardware and research documentation — complete at the current evidence level.** Aircraft integration is documented; final payload details remain pending.
4. **First flight and log processing — recorded.** The 28 September flight has an import pipeline and derived analysis. It is not a repeatability or payload-validation campaign.
5. **Conditional RTL comparison — packaged.** The current model, frozen inputs, native-time references, metrics and visual evidence are included. The 8/10 goal, whole-flight transfer and independent-flight prediction remain unresolved.
6. **Improve and independently evaluate the aircraft model — future.** Establish propulsion/transient evidence, justify transition-state handling, freeze parameters and evaluate a new flight without choosing settings on its evaluation output.
7. **Complete environmental payload — future.** Finish the mount/inlet; measure final mass and centre of gravity; establish timestamped payload logging and bench calibration.
8. **Sampling research — future.** Evaluate placement bias and repeatability before vertical profiles, sparse 3-D mapping and two-flight resampling.

Each result needs its configuration, processing method, quantitative criteria
and limitations. A loaded configuration must not inherit performance claims
from an earlier unloaded flight. The blank [flight templates](../flight-testing/README.md)
remain available for subsequent tests.
