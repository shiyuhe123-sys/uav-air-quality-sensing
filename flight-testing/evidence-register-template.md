Use one row for each claim or observation. An entry marked private may be referenced by a non-sensitive identifier, but the underlying evidence still requires review. ## Register guidance

- Use a stable, non-sensitive evidence identifier.
- Link a claim to the exact configuration and test record used to produce it.
- Identify the raw log by provenance without publishing it automatically.
- Link any analysis script, table or figure and record its limitations.
- Mark evidence as pending until the configuration, test record and analysis have been reviewed.
- Use `private` for evidence that may be retained locally but must not be published; do not include its precise location in the public repository.
- Do not use the register to turn a planned test, completed checklist or simulation result into a physical-flight result.
